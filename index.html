03 / THE POINT OF VIEW

Opinionated visuals.
Unopinionated code.

BrutalKit should make your interface more distinct without making your codebase harder to own.

01

No global takeover.

No global reset or broad element styling. Your app keeps control of its base styles.

02

CSS does the heavy lifting.

Use the stylesheet on its own. The optional JavaScript is limited to specific interactions.

03

Accessible foundations.

Visible focus, reduced-motion handling, semantic patterns, and keyboard-aware interactions.

04

Make it yours.

Customize colors, borders, shadows, radii, typography, and spacing with CSS variables.
